## Summary (2026-10-15)

Five games: four reached stages 17–25, the best run since round 4 (38,620,
stage 25), and one ended at stage 9. **Nobody reached Neptune.** A run to
Neptune is 3 stages to the first station plus six legs: about 21–27 stages
on normal routes, 27–33 on safe ones. Run 4 (stage 23, safe and normal) was
close.

**The stage 9 wall is gone.** Round 7 lost four of five runs at the stage 9
swarm; this round only one run ended there.

**Late in a run, the ship runs short of pickups and coins.** Runs 1–2,
before loot by danger, got several upgrades. Run 4, after it, took safe
routes and could afford only two upgrades and ran out of power-ups near the
end. That's the trade-off working as designed (safe routes are easier but
send at most one item a wave, at 4.5% instead of 6%, and pay 2 coins a stage
instead of 3), but it may be too harsh: the safe route is the one a
struggling player picks, and it leaves them poorer for the hardest regions.

For scale: every upgrade tier costs about 420 coins in all, a repair 15, an
item 6–10. Stage clears pay 2–6 coins, so most coins come from coin
pickups, and the game didn't record how many. **Fixed for round 9: the end
screen now shows coins earned and spent, upgrades bought, and routes taken
by danger.**

**Done since:** the line along the path taken on the map (run 3's request).

**To decide before round 9** (see
[design-notes.md](design-notes.md#playtest-round-8-findings-2026-10-15)):

- **Safe routes too poor?** Decided 2026-10-16: safe routes send items
  like normal routes (up to 2 a wave, 6%) and keep only their coins
  lower. Adjust after round 9.
- **The gates:** Phase 3's gate is that a strong player needs the HUD to get
  past stage 20. Three runs passed 20; note in round 9 whether the HUD was
  used to do it.
